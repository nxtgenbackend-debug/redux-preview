Why the finish looks different

Prep is the job. The spray gun just finishes it.

A brush leaves ridges and uneven sheen on the surfaces you see up close — cabinet doors, front doors, trim. Spraying lays one continuous film, so the result looks finished rather than painted. But it only works on a surface that has been prepared properly.

  1. Protect the houseFloors are covered before anything starts, and the house is cleaned up every evening.
  2. Sand, fill and deglossOld finish is scuffed back, dents and gaps are filled, and glossy surfaces are deglossed so the new coat bonds.
  3. Prime properlyA proper primer coat, so the finish has something sound to hold on to.
  4. Spray in placeA portable spray booth goes up inside your home. Cabinet doors are finished on site and never leave the house.

Questions

What people usually ask

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size and condition of the work, so there’s no flat price list. Every estimate is free, written, itemized and fixed. As a guide, cabinet painting starts from $2,500.

How quickly can you come out?

Call or send the estimate form. You can email photos for a ballpark before the visit. In Glendale, a walkthrough is usually possible within a couple of days.

Are you insured?

Yes. Documentation is provided before any work starts.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes, a 1-year written warranty on all work. The terms are on the estimate.

Do my cabinet doors leave the house?

No. They’re sprayed in a portable booth set up in your home. Expect about a week of open shelving, and the central air is switched off while spraying.

What’s the 2K upgrade?

Renner Aquaris, a waterborne 2K catalyzed polyurethane. It cures harder than the standard cabinet finish (Sherwin-Williams Emerald Urethane Trim Enamel). It costs more and is quoted as its own line, so you can decide.

Do I need to be home?

For the walkthrough, yes. While the work is being done, that’s up to you.

Do you take small jobs?

Yes. A single door or one piece of furniture is a normal job.

How long is my front door out of action?

Most single doors are done in a day and can be closed that evening. A full cure takes several days.

When is exterior season?

Roughly May through October. Book early, especially near the lake.
